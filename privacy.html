Privacy Policy

Regulatory Docket · Last updated October 13, 2026

Regulatory Docket has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. Stories you save for later, your watchlist and your reminders are stored only on your device. Reminders are scheduled by your phone itself; the app does not register your device with a notification server.

If you subscribe, the purchase is handled by Apple or Google. We never see your name, email or payment details. To confirm that a subscription is active, the app uses a random identifier created on your device, which is shared with our subscription provider, RevenueCat, together with your purchase history for this app.

When you open the app it downloads the latest brief from a public server. Like any internet request, the server may log technical data such as your IP address for security and reliability. The app does not use this to identify you.

Links open the original regulator and news websites, which have their own privacy practices.

The brief is general information, not legal advice.
