Contact

Email arcaneartificer94@gmail.com with questions, problems or corrections. Please include the edition date and the story headline if you are reporting an error.

Corrections

Every story links to the primary release it is based on, and that source always governs. If you spot an error, tell us and we will check it against the source and correct the edition.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are bought and billed through the App Store or Google Play. To cancel or change one, open your store account settings and choose Subscriptions. If you reinstall the app or change phones, use Restore purchases on the Account tab.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. If they do not arrive, check that notifications are allowed for Regulatory Docket in your phone's settings.

Not legal advice

The brief is general information for counsel. It is not legal advice and does not create a lawyer-client relationship.
